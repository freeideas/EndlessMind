Endless MindDocs

Anything AI can make, connected into one open network.

AI coding agents let anyone make a game just by describing it. Endless Mind connects them, the way the web connects websites, with no company in the middle. You are the same player everywhere, and what you earn in one realm you can show in another, with proof.

How it works, in short

  • Your secret phrase is you. 24 ordinary words, written down once. No account, password or email address.
  • It never leaves your device. A small page called an EntryPortal, usually on your phone, proves you have your phrase without showing it to anyone. Anyone can host one, and any AI helper can check that it is safe.
  • Sign in by scanning a code. A realm (a game, a place, anything you can enter) shows a QR code, your phone scans it, and you are in.
  • Records you can show anywhere. Achievements and trades are signed by everyone involved, so nobody can fake them, and your reputation can only grow.
  • Found by real play, not likes. Boards, which anyone can run, rank realms by public records of time actually played.

Try it: Endless Maze is a small example realm. Read the design and protocol for the rest.

Endless Mind™ is an open protocol, and nothing depends on this website. Source and docs: github.com/freeideas/EndlessMind.
