# The Endless Mind name

The code is open (MIT or Apache 2.0) and the protocol documents are in the public domain (CC0). The **name** "Endless Mind" is the one thing kept back, only so that people can trust what carries it. This policy says what anyone may do with it.

## You may, without asking

- Build anything that works with Endless Mind: portals, servers, realms, renderers, directories, tools.
- Say so plainly: "works with Endless Mind", "an Endless Mind portal", "speaks the Endless Mind protocol", "a realm on Endless Mind".
- Use the name in articles, tutorials, videos, talks and reviews.
- Fork the code and change it however you like.

## Please do not

- Name your own product, fork, company or service "Endless Mind", or something easily confused with it, so that people think it is the official one.
- Suggest that your product or realm is official, endorsed or certified when it is not.
- Use the name for anything that deceives people, such as fake downloads or scams.

A fork is welcome; it just needs its own name.

## Why

The name is how a newcomer finds the real reference portal and documents. Keeping it from being used by impostors protects actors, not control: the protocol works the same no matter whose software speaks it, and no one needs permission to build on it.

Questions: open an issue in the repository.
